A version string or historical tag alone selects none of those records.

## Scientific operation ceilings

The [system inventory below](#systems-and-current-states) represents current systems and historical results.
The [route register](substrate-route-register.md) retains 93 source-register entries and their distinct dispositions.
Reactor and six-matrix response are the bounded first-release family integration scope.
Other unfinished integrations are deferred.
Checked native development starts remain available only at their documented ceilings.

The reactor prefix has development authoring and a separately assigned source-bound lifecycle.
Its public example remains exposed.
Response-route input checks require exact held source and, where applicable, manifested upstream port contexts.
They do not qualify the historical full cohorts.

Prepared, information and causal response can compile development candidates from their held plans and fitted banks. Dependent refinement requires a qualified common prepared charter. Fresh calibration requires a nominated qualified refinement library. The authentic negative prepared screen supplies neither parent.

Finite calibration and evaluation need distinct complete assignments and exact authenticated parents. Continuation preserves the original evaluation roots. Post-hoc response composition may use its declared negative parent under separate analysis authority.

Circuit and electron-gas development candidates have selected source and evaluator bindings. The lattice-pairing candidate has a synthetic producer and evaluator. Those public deterministic references establish no new physical or material independent unit. Native tokamak, battery, neuron, flow, reaction-field and grid diagnostics have their stated local development checks.

They supply no general campaign qualification. The laser import is retrospective and lacks the guarded target publication handoff. The material preflight examines supplied envelopes without executing a solver or qualifying material.

Prospective physical RC, inverter, building-control and external electron-material routes were removed from first-release scope.
Their remaining historical or synthetic records do not supply native researcher authority.
Quantum, static-equilibrium, biological and other retained routes keep the stops stated in the register.
A missing prerequisite remains absent after dependency installation or static discovery.

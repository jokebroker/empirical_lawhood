## Scientific question and discrimination

Can a bounded upstream preparation make unchanged F valid and useful where a
competent equal-duration waiting comparator leaves it invalid? F's complete
payload, support box, action chart, receiver, 192-tick horizon, calibration,
uncertainty and refusal semantics remain fixed. The lower payload hash is
`ab880c96a5e4d4e5070e4e2268331ea542f5cc0bedf18da43cded949d2462b1b`.

applicability, while active preparation only shifts continuous margins or repairs
isolated cases that cannot be identified at the permitted causal cutoff. The
missing discriminating operand is a prospectively identifiable incoming failure
population with actual useful service available after repair. Neither another
unselected panel nor more requests nested within the same roots supplies it.

## Fresh experiment entry contract

Proceed once a concrete incoming population can be frozen that has measured
recoverable failure and service headroom. Two scientifically different scopes
need an explicit choice: retain the existing source population and accept the
rare effect, or construct a new boundary-challenge population and qualify its
denominator and compatibility. Construction must describe native preparation
instances and independent acquisition units; cloning one exposed prefix cannot
be counted as independent preparations.

For that fresh identity, freeze incoming inclusion using observations available
before preparation, independently assigned source innovations, and all screened
and failed units. Pair N with equal-duration HOLD, using the strongest relevant
existing preparation as an additional comparator where distinct. Observe the
actual handoff and seal unchanged-F lower choices before any lower response.
Assess full applicability and covered useful service separately, including
preparation cost, numerical discrepancy, failed observations and NONATTEMPTs.
Do not require adaptive superiority to establish a bounded fixed-preparation
production effect. Any adaptive claim needs its own best-fixed comparison.

Freeze exact unit counts, effect resolution, confidence rule, resource bounds,
inclusion/selection, authority and conditional qualification stages before fresh
outcomes. Reuse the current public candidate/V2 issue/V8/V5 execution/reveal route
and the existing CC1 native and scientific owners through new typed bindings.
Prove the full no-contact route before issue. This audit cannot substitute for
that proof, source qualification, fresh response evidence or law adjudication.

An N support crossing without full validity is partial repair. Full validity
without improved covered service is applicability production without established
service benefit. Positive prospective paired improvement with unchanged F
supports the specified bounded production claim; negative or unresolved effects
retain those statuses. Whole-composition qualification and further consumer use
remain conditional on this useful dependency and separate declared evidence.

“Before model fitting, calculate all model-independent opportunity conditions”.

negative scientific result about all preparations. It does not establish that
the fresh experiment has been executed or that the frontier is impossible.

replay, law refitting or retrospective repair of completed results belongs here.

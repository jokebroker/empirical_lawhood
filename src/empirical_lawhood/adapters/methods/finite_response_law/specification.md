### What closes a milestone

Keep three separate facts in the existing stage ledger/assessment.
Do not build a new platform gate framework to express them:

1. **Technical readiness:** required tests, bindings, authority, resources and
   exact production proof passed. This permits the next authorized operation.
2. **Execution and evaluability:** the declared analysis/acquisition was
   actually performed, its full denominator and missingness accounted for,
   required external receipts recovered, and scientific adjudication/readout
   completed. An evaluator must be able to reproduce its decision from those
   scientific artifacts and the frozen specification without consulting a
   “components complete” checklist or test-success count.
3. **Scientific conclusion:** the exact claim is supported, not supported,
   mixed or unevaluable, and each subsequent operation's eligibility follows from the
   declared scientific predicates. Preserve separate conclusions per claim.

A valid evaluable negative can complete a scientific milestone and close its
descendants. An implementation/authority/resource blockage is an execution
blockage, not a negative experimental result. An unevaluable outcome can be
honestly adjudicated and operationally closed, but the affected scientific
question remains unanswered. Do not call either category successful scientific
execution, or map it to `NOT_SUPPORTED` merely to terminate the work queue.
Apply this distinction per claim. Keep the predeclared conservative missing-outcome failures in binary consumer success/risk.

A missing response panel can leave the separate prediction-loss comparison unevaluable.
Never use one claim's missing-data convention to invent another claim's result.

The bounded protocol is fully executed only when all these conditions hold:

- Every entered stage has its required terminal evidence/readout.
- All eligible stages are resolved.
- Every unentered descendant is accounted for by a valid predeclared scientific stop.


Where resources, missing authority or a platform defect prevent this, report
the achieved scientific conclusions and incomplete/blocked remainder explicitly.
Administrative closeout is still required. It is not fulfillment of an
unexecuted scientific objective. Positive hypothesis support is never required
to count an otherwise valid negative experiment as completed science.

## 1. Scientific contract and claims

The tested chain is

`pre-parent observation z0 -> U(z0,parent) -> frozen F(z, native word, 192)`

`-> simultaneous paired-response/preservation bounds -> two finite consumers`.

The lower law is parent-free. The description may include curvature and a
past-only local rate. The actuator remains finite. The external receiver is an
explicit common-start signed-pair assay. A successful result is a bounded law
and composition/reuse result in this prepared simulator population. It does
not establish arbitrary forces, a single-run physical target controller,
arbitrary action composition, measured phase coordinates, or universal lawhood.

Keep these verdicts separate in the machine readout:

| Flag | Evidence required | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| `TASK_OPPORTUNITY` | Native low/high words differ under the fixed requests, adequate oracle contact | Current-interface information helps |
| `COMPOSED_USE_NOMINATED` | Held-root `F(U)` bounds support both actual consumers with adequate success/risk | Prospective qualification |
| `INFORMATION_NOMINATED` | Held-root composed response loss improves on the cached parent mean by the declared amount | Added consumer benefit |
| `LOWER_QUALIFIED` | The sole qualification service accepts the exact finite lower payload, source and calibration evidence | Pre-parent composition or use succeeds |
| `CONSUMER_USE_SUPPORTED` | Fresh all-assigned-root joint success and false-admission criteria pass | The enriched predictor is necessary |
| `CONSUMER_INFORMATION_SUPPORTED` | Fresh primary composed-versus-mean prediction comparison passes | Different actions or better task success |
| `CONSUMER_ADDED_USE_SUPPORTED` | Fresh paired consumer-success comparison against the mean passes | Preparation improves this law |
| `PREPARATION_ADEQUACY_SUPPORTED` | Target-blind preparation beats the frozen best-fixed schedule on actual unchanged-law adequacy | Downstream usefulness improves |
| `PREPARATION_STRONG_SUPPORTED` | Adequacy and downstream-use improvements pass, with selected-policy absolute success/risk gates | Adequacy and use occurred on the same episodes |
| `PREPARATION_JOINED_USE_SUPPORTED` | The stronger prospectively ordered comparison of `C=A AND J` passes, in addition to `PREPARATION_STRONG_SUPPORTED` | Causal mediation, intrinsic accuracy improvement, or any wider recursive-lawhood claim |

Do not compress these flags to one favorable status. A simple mean may support
the consumers as well as the enriched package. That is a valid simple-interface
result. A direct predictor can outperform composition without invalidating
reuse that independently passes. Negative, mixed, stopped and unevaluable
outcomes are terminal within this tranche.

### Adequacy, successful use and their actual join

For root r and preparation policy p, define the **full-menu** lower adequacy
event `A[r,p] = S AND V AND E AND W AND P AND K`:

| Conjunct | Exact scope |
|---|---|
| S | Primary measured handoff is in the frozen lower feature/support domain. |
| V | All required signed-mate/HOLD/source identities and 128 outcome cells for this root/parent are authenticated, available and finite. Every per-cell primary/refined discrepancy is at most nu. |
| E | The one primary-handoff lower prediction table has absolute error at most delta against every required pair/output/future/view. |
| W | Every lower **nominal expanded half-width** `q_lower*sigma+nu` is at most delta. Domain clipping cannot make this predicate pass. |
| P | Both signed branches of all four pairs satisfy all preservation caps in both futures/views. |
| K | Measured parent work is at most 32 in both views. |

Define `J[r,p]` as the existing all-assigned joint A/B episode success below,
and **`C[r,p]=A[r,p] AND J[r,p]`**. Compute all three for selected, best-fixed
and HOLD from already planned panels. The provider continues to predict A.
It is not refitted to C or given requests. The scalar provider scores are
uncalibrated selection scores, distinct from U2's interface predictions and
its calibrated composed response box.

Store the complete six-component Boolean vector and every changed conjunct
for each A discordance. Also report continuous errors, coverage, widths,
preservation and work. A work/support/preservation improvement can improve A
without improving prediction accuracy. A first-failure label cannot replace
this decomposition. Record selected-pair versions of S/V/E/W/P/K for diagnostic
comparison only. They cannot substitute for the frozen full-menu A or C.

Separate improvements in A and J do not imply an improvement in C, or even
one adequate-and-successful episode. The prospective Tier 2 comparison therefore retains the narrower
flags and adds a predeclared C comparison for the joined claim. Its meaning is
increased incidence of successful use within the declared adequate-law domain,
not proof that adequacy causally mediates the use benefit. If the stronger endpoint has low power, do not add a C-based development entry gate. Do not replace A with task-restricted adequacy. Do not enlarge n.

### Precision, calibration and support

The fixed error and **nominal expanded half-width** limits, in output order,
are `delta=(0.01,0.01,0.0125,0.005,0.005,0.0125,0.005,0.005)`.
These are synthetic assay precision requirements: response precision is tied
to the narrowest transverse bound and preservation precision to one tenth of
its cap. They are not the abandoned 5% command tolerance.

Use `nu_j=delta_j/8`. For every required output, compare the primary and
refined values at the same pair/purpose and require discrepancy <=nu_j.
This is a two-resolution self-consistency requirement, not an independently
proved error bound relative to exact continuous-time stochastic dynamics.
Do not estimate a larger floor from adverse outcomes or add a resolution wave.

Every predictor uses **one primary-input mean table against both numerical
views and both futures**. Lower F uses the primary measured handoff. Composed,
cached and direct tables use their permitted primary pre-parent inputs.
There is no refined-handoff refit/re-evaluation in the primary lower score.
For preservation, clamp the point mean to zero before any residual, loss,
scale or bound calculation. After numerical expansion, intersect the interval
with `[0,infinity)`.

Response coordinates are never clamped. Keep the nominal
half-width `h=q*sigma+nu` for precision tests even when the domain intersection
shortens a preservation interval. This one convention applies in development,
calibration, admission, evaluator readout and the independent checker.

Keep the following predicates distinct:

| Predicate | Frozen rule and scope |
|---|---|
| Statistical calibration | A finite nonnegative q from all 32 assigned root scores, with the fixed complete chart and no fitting on calibration outcomes. |
| Global lower precision W | All four pairs/eight outputs satisfy h<=delta at the actual primary handoff. This is a component of full-menu A, not a population-wide prerequisite for every qualified package. |
| Selected-word precision | All eight oriented outputs for the proposed pair satisfy the consuming boundary's h<=delta. Required for each A/B admission in every phase. An unused wide pair need not force refusal of a precise pair. |
| Task containment | The entire expanded/domain-intersected selected-word box lies inside the consumer's response and preservation constraints. This does not imply selected-word precision. |
| Support | Native identity/domain checks and the boundary-specific training-only feature rule below. No outcome-defined support region or comparison-population filtering. |
| Operational validity | Actual selected-pair/source/numerical/preservation/work/delivery conditions determine J and false admission. Early prediction cannot know later work failure or turn a cancellation into success. |

The raw data mask and endpoint rules are in section 3. Let V(r,p) denote a
complete authenticated finite 128-cell root-parent panel and per-cell numerical
agreement. Let S_B(r,p) be the corresponding boundary's support at its permitted
primary input. Define the finite point score

`T_B(r,p)=max_{k,j,f,v} max(abs(Y[r,p,k,j,f,v]-mu_B[r,p,k,j])-nu_j,0) / sigma_B[r,p,k,j]`.

If V or S_B fails, the **calibration/development score** for that root/parent
is infinity. Keep the root in the census. Actual preservation exceeding a cap
is still a measured response, not missing data. Do not make its score infinite
merely because it exceeds the cap. Parent-work excess similarly affects
operational use/A, not the residual score, when the handoff and outcomes exist.
Use the stage's declared maximum over parent/policy slots to obtain T_B(root).

Reversing orientation adds no new observation or calibration event.

For 32 fresh calibration roots, q is the 30th order statistic of all scores:
`ceil((32+1)*0.90)=30`. More generally, use rank `ceil((n+1)*0.90)`. When this rank exceeds n, use q=infinity. Never clip an out-of-range rank down to n. Do not use an interpolated percentile. Infinite scores remain in the sort.

Final nominal
half-width is `q*sigma_B(x)[k,j]+nu_j`, using the exact scales defined below.

A calibration result has 90% **marginal root** scope for its complete finite
vector under the fixed calibration/evaluation procedure and exchangeability.
It is not 90% conditional coverage at each interface. It is not a 90% lower population confidence bound for this realized package. It is not a guarantee conditional on the batch passing the usability filter. Nor do separately calibrated lower and
composed boundaries have an automatic joint 90% guarantee. Fresh Phase 4 use
and risk are independent claims with their own inference.

Do not add a fresh
tranche merely to strengthen calibration wording.

### Two-future diagnostics, without another gate

For each response/output coordinate and numerical view, use the same committed
prediction mu to report the exact decomposition

`((y1-mu)^2+(y2-mu)^2)/2 = (mean(y1,y2)-mu)^2 + (y1-y2)^2/4`.

Under conditionally iid futures, `(y1-y2)^2/2` is an unbiased conditional
variance estimate. Two futures do not estimate each root's variance precisely.
Report the frequency of `abs(y1-y2)>2*delta_j`: no common point prediction can
then satisfy both error limits, regardless of the fitted model. Because the
primary analysis also uses a common prediction across views, the wider
`max_{f,v}(Y)-min_{f,v}(Y)>2*delta_j` obstruction is valid as a separate
diagnostic. Missing quantities produce an unavailable diagnostic, not zero
spread. Neither diagnostic introduces an entry gate, tolerance change or new
acquisition.

This distinguishes realized future spread from point-fit error
without claiming nu bounds the true numerical solution error.

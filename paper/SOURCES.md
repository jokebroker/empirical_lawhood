The [documentary provenance register](source/evidence-manifest.json) retains original names, sizes and hashes for these external sources. Earlier paper payloads and editorial history are excluded from the public release. Their absence here does not mean the preserved original archive is absent or that the published references have changed.

The [claim-to-evidence inventory](source/claim-evidence-inventory.json) covers all 22 completed study/analysis families reported by the selected preprint, including retrospective and exploratory analyses. It distinguishes documentary presence, inspected external records, uninspected deeper native stores and unresolved producing-source or custody bindings. The external maintainer inventory retains exact original locators and hashes without disclosing private storage paths here. Availability is not a scientific verdict, and an uninspected archive is not evidence that a record is absent.

The public source enables [new experiments and results](../docs/fresh-experiment.md). Historical replay is a separate later programme. Reproduction on exposed cohorts and fresh prospective repetition preserve different protocols and evidence ceilings. Project-authored prose and figures use CC-BY-4.0; software uses MPL-2.0; third-party material retains its own terms.
